# 🚦 SmartRoute AI — Next-Gen AI Navigation & Traffic Intelligence Platform

**SmartRoute AI** is an intelligent, real-time route optimization and traffic management platform built with React, Vite, Express, and Google Gemini AI. It empowers drivers and city planners with predictive traffic insights, AI voice control, road hazard reporting, and eco-friendly route planning.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🧠 **AI-Powered Route Optimization**: Intelligent multi-route suggestion engine considering real-time traffic congestion, toll costs, fuel consumption, and weather conditions.
- 🔮 **Predictive Traffic Analytics**: Machine learning models and Gemini AI forecasts traffic bottlenecks before they occur.
- 🎙️ **Voice Command & Live AI Assistant**: Hands-free navigation control powered by Google Gemini Live AI & voice recognition.
- ⚠️ **Road Hazard & Incident Reporting**: Live alerts for speed cameras, police patrols, potholes, and construction zones.
- ⚡ **Nearby EV & Emergency Services**: Real-time locator for EV charging hubs, parking, fuel stations, and SOS emergency assistance.
- 📊 **Admin Monitoring & Analytics**: Comprehensive dashboard for municipal traffic controllers and fleet managers.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Lucide React, Motion, D3.js
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express
- **AI Integration**: Google Gemini AI (`@google/genai`)
